## Corpus qualification and replay evidence

Use `interlinked harness corpus status --json` to inspect all stored records and `interlinked harness corpus scan <check-id...> --json` to rescan 1–20 registered checks. With no IDs, scan runs the three qualification candidates. Scans execute raw detectors under `src`, exclude fixture directories explicitly, include tests for applicable detectors, and retain dirty input digests, detector/config identity, counts, failures and prior records. Missing paths, zero eligible files, thrown detectors or input races cannot qualify. Historical v1 records remain readable but unqualified.

Only `corpus_cohort` members in `.interlinked/check-evidence-baseline.json` acquire the corpus obligation. The initial enabled members are `async_promise_executor` and `nan_comparison`. A complete zero-hit run also needs positive, negative and sensitivity controls. `unawaited_async_assertion` is withheld while its retained single-line positive control is missed. Removing a cohort member is a baseline weakening requiring review; adding one must be accompanied by fresh evidence. The contract test scans the current cohort in memory on every local, pre-push and CI run without rewriting the historical store. No preparatory scan is needed after a clean edit. Stored result digests bind hits and occurrences; they detect inconsistent edits but are not authority signatures. Historical records without these bindings stay unqualified. Fresh hits still require review.

`interlinked tests e2e attempts <cohort> --json` joins stored attempt metadata and receipts. `tests e2e replay <cohort> <attempt> --timeout 120000 --json` reuses the seed/clock against matching declared inputs and runtime. Changed inputs are refused. Replay publishes diagnostic receipts and replay records without appending acceptance-ledger rows, reconciling obligations or serving open requests. A passing replay leaves the failed obligation unchanged. Reproduced, not-reproduced, diverged and inconclusive are observation outcomes; external environment closure is not certified. Mixed varied-seed attempts are not proof of flakiness.

The fast-check wrapper binds replay to engine/runtime, test source, lockfile and an optional explicit generator version. It is a candidate cache, not a portable counterexample across arbitrary helper/environment changes. `INTERLINKED_FC_EXAMPLES=off` prevents persistence and pruning; malformed stores are retained, dynamic titles are not pruned, and passing replay archives “not reproduced”. Writes use a bounded cross-process lock and preserve failure history. Fixed CI seeds have a separate `INTERLINKED_FC_EXPLORE=1` cadence. Promote confirmed failures to durable requirement-based tests.

The weekly evidence audit is advisory, owned by the repository maintainer, bounded to 15 minutes of verification with 14-day artifacts. Inspect NOT CHECKED and incomplete output before interpreting finding counts. Lexical property-weakening candidates failed paired-intent calibration, so this work adds no pre_warn/pre_block policy for them. Skill tokens are diagnostic (`python3 scripts/skill-token-cost.py`), with no hard budget.

`INTERLINKED_FC_EXPLORE=1` bypasses the fixed CI seed, including an explicit `INTERLINKED_FC_CI_SEED`. The weekly exploration step visibly fails on failed tests; an always-run summary consumes its JSON and reports passed, failed or unavailable. A missing or zero-test report is not success. The workflow is an advisory lane, not a required merge gate.
